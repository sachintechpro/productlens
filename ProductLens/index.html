01 / PRODUCT CONTEXT

One portal. Competing priorities.

A self-service insurance portal must reduce unnecessary service interactions while meeting security, accessibility, operational and customer-experience expectations.

Customer problemToo many routine requests require assistance.

Account access, policy information, vehicle changes and billing drive repeat contacts.

Product objectiveEnable more tasks to finish independently.

Improve journey completion and reduce avoidable customer effort without sacrificing safeguards.

ConstraintLimited release capacity and prerequisites.

Foundational access must precede dependent self-service journeys. Mandatory work has a target date.

03 / ROADMAP & TRADE-OFFS

Capacity determines commitments.

Adjust story-point capacity for each release. Features require their prerequisites to ship in an earlier release; anything that cannot fit remains visibly unallocated.

Story points represent relative delivery effort. They are not interchangeable with hours and have no guaranteed conversion to elapsed time.

Decisions and follow-up

  1. Validate product demand using customer research and observed service data.
  2. Confirm delivery estimates, dependencies, and delivery-team capability.
  3. Secure security, accessibility, legal, and operational approvals as applicable.
  4. Confirm actual adoption, outcomes and cost assumptions before financial authorization.
  5. Set release success metrics and a post-launch measurement plan.
